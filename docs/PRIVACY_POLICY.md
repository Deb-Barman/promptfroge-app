# Privacy Policy — Prompt Forge

_Draft: have it reviewed and add your contact details before publishing._

Prompt Forge stores everything you write as files in a folder on your device that you choose or create. The app has no
account system, no backend, no analytics, no advertising and no network access, and it does not collect, transmit or
share any personal data. App preferences (such as the vault location and theme) are stored locally on the device.

Files leave the device only when you explicitly use Share or Export; those go to the app or folder you select.
Because your vault is an ordinary folder, any backup or cloud-sync tool you apply to that folder is outside the app's control.
